## Suggested Functional Requirements

### FR1 — Stock Validation
The system shall validate that a requested stock ticker exists in the supported stock reference list.

### FR2 — Buy Stock
The system shall allow a user to buy a positive number of shares at a positive price and maintain the weighted average purchase price.

### FR3 — Sell Stock
The system shall allow a user to sell shares only when sufficient holdings exist and shall calculate realized P&L.

### FR4 — Portfolio Analytics
The system shall calculate invested value, current value, unrealized P&L, and realized P&L.

## Non-functional Requirements

- Performance
